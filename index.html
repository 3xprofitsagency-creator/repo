— Our Philosophy

"We do not make furniture.
We make heirlooms
that happen to be useful."

Alabis was founded on a conviction that Nigerian homes deserve furniture made with the same care and rigour as the finest European ateliers — but with materials, motifs, and a spirit that is entirely our own.

Every piece is made by hand in our Lagos workshop. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just wood, leather, linen, and decades of craft.
