Our Promise

The Art of
Deliberate
Making

Every Alabis piece begins as a conversation between master craftsman and raw material. Our Lagos atelier works exclusively with sustainably-harvested hardwoods, hand-selected stone, and textiles that earn their place through texture rather than trend.

We do not rush. A single dining table may take twelve weeks from first sketch to final finish — and that time is precisely what you are investing in.
